**Short answer:** Social justice frameworks reveal how placement systems can reproduce or disrupt inequities. This book chapter examines how writing program administrators can align placement practices with equity goals.

## Key findings

- Examines directed self-placement through a social justice lens
- Builds on the empirical findings from the DSP equity and student reflections studies
- Offers frameworks for WPAs to evaluate and reform placement practices
- Connects institutional policy decisions to student outcomes

## Publication

Tinkle, T., Godfrey, J., Hammond, J. W., & Moos, A. (2024). In *Social Justice WPAing*. Book chapter.
