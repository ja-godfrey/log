I speak about the systems behind educational improvement: how we measure implementation, make administrative data usable, and design fairer ways to learn from writing and student choices.

The catalogue below includes all **53 presentation records in my vita**, including talks for which no slide deck survives and two 2020 events disrupted by the pandemic. Links are described according to what they establish: an **official session** supports the session details shown; an **event page** establishes the event but not necessarily my participation; and **related work** documents the research rather than the talk. A collaborator credit does not by itself identify who presented.

Slide files will appear only after review for accuracy, rights, collaborator attribution, and accessibility. None are published on this page yet.
